What AlphaFlux does

AlphaFlux is the front office for service businesses. It answers your phones around the clock, captures every lead, books jobs onto your schedule, sends invoices and takes payments, and keeps your crew and your customers in the loop, so no call, lead, or job slips through the cracks. AlphaFlux plans are monthly subscriptions, and annual billing lowers the per-month price.
